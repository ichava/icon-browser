# Imported root files

`react-icon-browser/` holds the root files of `ichava/react-icon-browser` as they were at
`3cdbd1bc8689bd29772b50bbe3a4585112497fd2`, imported as a snapshot with no history on 2026-11-12
(react consolidation 3d). Its `src/` is `resources/js/`, its `scripts/` is `.dev/browser/` and its
fonts are `resources/assets/fonts/`; what is left here is kept, not built: the standalone and
embedded pages, its configs, docs, e2e suite and visual baselines, and its package manifest.

Its `LICENSE` is that repository's and is superseded here by this repository's root
[LICENSE](../../LICENSE): the Untitled UI PRO components the browser uses are included in this
product under the Untitled UI licence (owner, 2026-11-12). The 20 Metronic paths react-icon-browser
holds were not imported, and its `.github/` was left out. `src/test/setup.ts` is the copy that was
merged by hand into `resources/js/test/setup.ts`.

Nothing here is removed without the owner's yes.
